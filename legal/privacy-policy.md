# Privacy Policy

<p class="policy-meta">Last updated: April 25, 2026</p>

This Privacy Policy explains how Openly ("Openly," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Openly mobile application and related pages that link to this Policy.

Openly is an iOS app for adults who want intentional conversations through Moments, requests, private chats, and mutual profile reveal. Openly is in pre-release, so some support channels, moderation tools, and operational details may change before wider public launch.

## 1. Quick Summary

- Openly is for adults 18 and older.
- We collect information needed to create profiles, show local and global Moments, support requests and chats, enable mutual reveal, send notifications, maintain safety, and operate the app.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.
- We use service providers such as Apple, Google, Firebase, Google Cloud, and push notification services to operate Openly.
- You can update certain profile information in the app and delete your profile where the deletion feature is available.
- Location access is permission-based and is used to support local discovery. Openly is not intended to track your live location in the background.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few ways: information you provide, information created through your use of Openly, information from authentication providers, and technical information generated by our service providers.

| Category | Examples | Why we collect it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account and authentication information | Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, authentication tokens handled by Apple, Google, and Firebase Authentication, and provider account details made available through sign-in | To create and secure your account, keep you signed in, prevent abuse, and support account deletion |
| Profile information | Username, birthday, age, gender selection, location label, latitude and longitude if you allow location access, profile photo, created and updated dates | To create your profile, enforce the 18+ requirement, show profile context, support local discovery, and enable reveal |
| User content | Moments, Moment text, opening questions, tags, mood, visual choices, optional emoji, optional images where available, interest answers, chat messages, reveal choices, request status, and similar content | To provide the core Openly experience, display content to intended audiences, support chats, and operate safety features |
| Location information | Current location permission status, coordinates returned by the device when you choose to set location, and a human-readable location name | To show local Moments and help members understand general proximity context |
| Photos and media | Profile photos and any images you choose to upload for Moments or other features | To show profile media when allowed by the app experience and to support content features |
| Notifications and device identifiers | Firebase Cloud Messaging token, Apple Push Notification token, platform, token update times, notification metadata | To send request and chat notifications and remove stale tokens |
| App activity and records | Moment creation, requests sent or received, accepted requests, chats, message counts, read status stored on device, reveal state, deletion requests, sign-out events, and support/safety records | To provide features, sync state, troubleshoot, enforce rules, and maintain safety |
| Technical and security data | Device, network, server, error, diagnostic, and usage information processed by Firebase, Google Cloud, Apple, Google, or app-store systems | To operate, secure, debug, improve, and protect Openly |
| Communications with us | Messages, requests, reports, feedback, and related contact details if you contact support or the project owner | To respond, investigate, enforce policies, and improve the service |

Openly does not ask for your phone contacts, payment card details, government ID, precise home address, or financial account information in the current app experience.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, and secure accounts.
- Confirm that members meet the 18+ requirement.
- Create and display profiles, Moments, requests, chats, and reveal states.
- Show local and global Moments.
- Process interest answers and accepted requests.
- Send push notifications about new requests and chats, if notifications are enabled.
- Store profile photos and other media you choose to upload.
- Maintain member safety, investigate abuse, enforce the Terms and Community Guidelines, and prevent fraud or spam.
- Debug, monitor, protect, and improve Openly.
- Respond to support requests and legal requests.
- Comply with law and protect rights, safety, and property.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

We share information in the following ways.

### With Other Members

Openly is a social app, so some information is shared with other members as part of the experience.

- Moments may be visible to signed-in members, including your username, age, gender, location label, Moment text, mood, tags, visual style, and opening question.
- Interest answers are shared with the member who created the Moment.
- Private chat messages are shared with the other chat participant.
- Your username may be visible in requests and chats.
- Profile photo and fuller profile details are intended to stay hidden until mutual reveal, but once revealed, the other participant can view them.
- Other members may copy, screenshot, save, or share information you disclose, even if Openly's rules prohibit misuse.

### With Service Providers

We use vendors and platform services to operate Openly. These may include:

- Apple services, including Sign in with Apple, App Store systems, and Apple Push Notification service.
- Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
- Firebase Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions for Firebase, and Google Cloud infrastructure.
- Hosting and repository services used to publish these policy pages.

These providers process information for app functionality, authentication, hosting, storage, notifications, security, diagnostics, and similar operational purposes. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

### For Safety, Legal, and Compliance Reasons

We may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with law, legal process, or enforceable government requests.
- Enforce our Terms or Community Guidelines.
- Detect, prevent, or address fraud, abuse, spam, security incidents, or safety risks.
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of Openly, members, service providers, or the public.
- Respond to emergencies or potential harm.

### Business Transfers

If Openly is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate protections where required.

### With Your Direction or Consent

We may share information when you direct us to do so or give consent.

## 5. Sensitive Information

Some information in Openly can be sensitive, including gender identity, birthday, age, location coordinates, profile photos, private messages, and content about personal experiences. We use this information to provide Openly, maintain safety, comply with law, and support member-requested features. We do not use sensitive information to sell ads or to profile you for third-party targeted advertising.

## 6. No Sale, No Targeted Advertising

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or third-party targeted advertising. We do not currently use advertising SDKs.

If that changes, we will update this Policy and provide any choices required by law before using information in that way.

## 7. Your Choices and Controls

You can control certain information through the app and your device settings.

- Profile: You may update editable profile fields such as username, location, and profile photo where the app allows it. Birthday, age, and gender may be locked after setup.
- Location: You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS settings. Without location, local discovery may be limited or unavailable.
- Photos: You can control photo library access in iOS settings. Profile photo functionality may require a selected photo.
- Notifications: You can allow, mute, or disable push notifications in iOS settings.
- Sign-out: You can sign out in the app.
- Delete profile: You can delete your profile in the app where available. Deletion is intended to remove your profile, Moments, requests, chats, messages, reveal states, push tokens, and uploaded profile media from active app systems, subject to retention described below.

## 8. Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Openly, maintain security and safety, comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and support legitimate operational purposes.

- Active profile information is retained while your account exists.
- Moments are designed to expire from active feeds after approximately 30 days unless deleted earlier or made inactive.
- Deleting an individual Moment may remove it from active display while related records may remain for safety, abuse prevention, backups, analytics-free operational records, or legal reasons.
- Deleting your profile is intended to remove your core profile, active Moments, requests, chats, messages, reveal states, push tokens, and uploaded media from active app systems.
- Backup copies, logs, provider records, and legal/safety records may persist for a limited period or as required by law.
- If account deletion requires recent authentication from Apple, Google, or Firebase, the app may ask you to sign in again before the authentication account can be fully removed.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. For example, Openly uses authenticated access, Firebase security rules, platform permission prompts, and service-provider infrastructure.

No app, network, database, or transmission method is perfectly secure. You should use strong device security, keep your Apple or Google account secure, and be careful about what you share with other members.

## 10. Children and Age Restrictions

Openly is not for children or teenagers under 18. We do not knowingly allow people under 18 to create profiles, and the app requires age information during setup.

If you believe someone under 18 is using Openly, contact us through the available support channel. If we learn that we collected information from a person under 18 in violation of this Policy, we may delete the account and related information.

## 11. U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and whether a particular privacy law applies to Openly, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or an explanation of how personal information is collected, used, and disclosed. You may also have the right to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain uses of sensitive personal information.

Openly does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use sensitive information only for purposes described in this Policy.

To exercise privacy rights, use the app tools available to you, including profile editing and profile deletion, or contact us through the support contact made available in the app, on the App Store product page, or through the official Openly repository while the app is in pre-release. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 12. International Use

Openly is operated using service providers that may process information in the United States and other countries. If you use Openly from outside your country, your information may be transferred to and processed in places with different data protection laws than where you live.

Based on the current Openly app, relevant data types may include identifiers, user content, photos, location, sensitive information, diagnostics, and other data linked to app functionality. This summary may change as features or third-party services change.
